Arcade starter

A local test bench for the Arcade API: design, product, price, checkout. Every call and its raw response is shown in the log.

1 Connection

Checks that the server has your key and that Arcade accepts it. Nothing is created.

2 Categories

What Arcade can manufacture. Print categories take any pattern and can be refined; jewelry categories design the piece and build its product in one step.

3 Generate a design

Takes one to two minutes. For jewelry, name the metal and details in the prompt.

4 Build a product print only

Uses the current pattern and the selected print category. Change an option and Arcade reports which other values can still be made.

5 Product and price

Arcade's product images, maker and guaranteed unit price.

No product yet.

6 Checkout link

Creates a real checkout on your Arcade account for the current product. Nothing is charged until someone pays on Arcade's page; this app never pays.
